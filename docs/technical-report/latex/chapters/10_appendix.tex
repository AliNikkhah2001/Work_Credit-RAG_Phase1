\texttt{LANGFUSE\_HOST} & \texttt{localhost:3001} & Langfuse v2; collector fallback is \texttt{:3000} \\
\texttt{KB\_DB\_URL} & \texttt{postgresql+asyncpg://…:5432/kb\_manager} & pgvector HNSW. Set explicitly so the vector index is the store in use \\
\texttt{KB\_EMBED\_MODEL} & \texttt{paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2} & 384-d dense leg \\
\texttt{KB\_RERANKER\_MODEL} & \texttt{BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3} & MiniLM mmarco is the lightweight fallback \\
\texttt{KB\_RERANK\_POOL} & 15 CPU / 30 GPU & Cross-encoder candidate cap \\
\texttt{UPSTREAM\_LLM\_BASE\_URL} & \texttt{localhost:9000/v1} & Guardrails dials Gemma through the H200 manager \\
\texttt{UPSTREAM\_LLM\_MODEL} & \texttt{gemma-4-31b} or the unsloth UD-Q4\_K\_XL id & Match the process actually listening \\
\texttt{CTX\_SIZE} & 8192 & Gemma context window \\
\texttt{GUARD\_JUDGE\_BACKEND} & \texttt{gemma} (default), \texttt{mizan}, or \texttt{hybrid} & Adjudicator \\
\texttt{GUARD\_JUDGE\_MODE} & \texttt{on-signal} (default) or \texttt{always} & Skip the LLM judge on clean text when on-signal \\
\texttt{GUARD\_NEMO\_SELFCHECK} & \texttt{on} / \texttt{shadow} / \texttt{off} & Selects the direct-LLM yes/no self-check \\
\texttt{HF\_HOME} and \texttt{HF\_HUB\_CACHE} & both set, same cache directory & Hub 1.x reads \texttt{\$HF\_HOME/hub}. Setting only one misses weights \\
\texttt{WEBUI\_AUTH} & \texttt{false} on the ICS Helper MVP & \texttt{true} when per-user ids are required \\
Studio run field \texttt{request\_id} & required on every Studio input & Joins the \texttt{:2024} thread to traces \\
\end{longtable}

\texttt{httpx} clients that call localhost also set \texttt{trust\_env=False} in code. Exporting \texttt{no\_proxy=localhost,127.0.0.1} does not replace that flag while Squid is at \texttt{192.168.203.2:3128}.
